# Responsible AI Considerations 🛡️

*Aligned with 1M1B – IBM SkillsBuild AI + Sustainability Virtual Internship Guidelines*

Every AI solution developed for sustainability must adhere to core ethical and responsible AI practices. WasteWise AI is engineered with four mandatory responsible AI pillars:

---

## 1. Fairness ⚖️
- **Packaging Diversity:** The AI vision prompts and classification schemas are designed to avoid regional bias, recognizing diverse waste shapes, languages on labels, crushed cans, and varied packaging materials across urban, campus, and rural environments.
- **Inclusive Categorization:** Allowed categories strictly map to universal recycling standards (`Plastic`, `Paper`, `Glass`, `Metal`, `Organic`, `E-Waste`, `General Waste`) rather than proprietary commercial formats.

---

## 2. Transparency 🔍
- **Clear AI Disclosure:** Users are explicitly informed that all classification results are generated by an Artificial Intelligence model.
- **Explainability Box:** On every scan result, WasteWise AI provides an Explainability Breakdown detailing:
  - Estimated Confidence percentage (e.g. 96%).
  - 1-3 visual evidence reasons (e.g., *"Cylindrical shape characteristic of beverage cans"*, *"Visible metallic sheen"*).
  - Exact model and version metadata.
- **Honest Feedback on Failure:** If confidence falls below 50% or the item is ambiguous, the system refuses to give deterministic disposal advice and marks it as *"Unable to identify confidently"*, prompting a clearer image.

---

## 3. Ethics & Safety 🌿
- **Non-Misleading Guidance:** The application never guarantees that an item is 100% accepted by every local plant; it highlights that local municipal municipal rules may vary.
- **Hazardous Material Safeguards:** For E-Waste, batteries, or unknown hazardous items, special disposal guidelines are strictly triggered, preventing users from putting dangerous items into general or compost bins.
- **Scientific Integrity:** The application does not fabricate fictitious carbon claims (e.g., "You saved 12 trees today!"). All environmental metrics are estimated on conservative empirical diversion rates.

---

## 4. Privacy & Data Protection 🔒
- **Targeted Data Collection:** Only images of waste items are processed. No biometric, facial, or personal identity data is requested or stored.
- **Complete Data Ownership & Deletion:** Users can permanently delete individual scans or wipe their entire account at any time. Account deletion executes a cascaded cleanup across MongoDB and Cloudinary CDN storage.
- **Secure Authentication:** Passwords are encrypted with Bcrypt (10 salt rounds) and session authentication is secured with JWT tokens and Helmet headers.
